HobbyAtlas

HobbyAtlas is a project manager built for hobbies: gardening, model building, electronics, woodworking, collections and personal projects. Each project gets a board, a materials list with a budget, a photo timeline and notes.

Your projects stay on this Mac. No account, no subscription, no paywall.

This installer puts HobbyAtlas in your Applications folder and opens it when it's done.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).
